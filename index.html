Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

NFC Tag Writer ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data processed by the App remains entirely on your device.

Camera Access

The App requests access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes and barcodes. Camera data is processed in real-time on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

NFC Access

The App uses NFC (Near Field Communication) capabilities to read from and write to NFC tags. NFC data is processed locally on your device and is not shared with any third parties.

Local Data Storage

The App stores your scan and operation history locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data includes:

  • Content of scanned QR codes and barcodes
  • Content read from or written to NFC tags
  • Generated QR codes and barcodes content
  • Timestamps of operations

This data is stored exclusively on your device and can be deleted at any time through the app's History section.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share any information with third parties.

Photos Library

When you choose to save a generated QR code or barcode image, the App accesses your Photos Library. This access is only used to save images at your explicit request.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App is rated 4+ and does not contain any age-restricted content.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, your information is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
